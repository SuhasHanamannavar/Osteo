The Problem We Solve

Osteoarthritis silently affects millions, but early detection remains out of reach for rural communities.

Late Diagnosis

By the time most patients in remote areas receive a diagnosis, the condition has progressed to irreversible stages, requiring costly joint replacements.

Limited Access

Remote villages lack X-ray machines and orthopedic specialists. Patients must travel hundreds of kilometers for basic evaluation.

Connectivity Gaps

Hilly regions suffer from poor internet connectivity, making cloud-based diagnostic tools unreliable when they're needed most.

Our Three-Pillar Solution

A complete ecosystem combining hardware, software, and artificial intelligence.

Pillar 01

Hardware

The Eyes & Ears

A small, battery-powered wearable band equipped with precision motion sensors. Wrapped around the patient's knee, it captures movement patterns as they walk.

  • Lightweight & portable design
  • Long-lasting battery power
  • Wireless data transmission
  • High-precision motion sensors
Pillar 02

Software

The Notebook & Messenger

A mobile app for health workers to record patient data, collect sensor readings, and securely store everything locally — even without internet.

  • Simple, intuitive interface
  • Offline-first data storage
  • Auto-sync when connectivity returns
  • Secure & encrypted patient records
Pillar 03

AI / ML

The Doctor's Brain

Trained on thousands of knee movement patterns, our AI analyzes gait data and delivers instant risk assessments to guide health workers.

  • Instant pattern recognition
  • Red / Yellow / Green risk alerts
  • Trained on diverse datasets
  • Continuously improving accuracy

How It Works

A seamless workflow from patient assessment to expert diagnosis.

01

Wear & Walk

Health worker places the sensor band on the patient's knee. The patient walks naturally as motion data is captured.

02

Record Data

The app receives sensor data wirelessly. Health worker enters patient details and pain levels into the mobile app.

03

AI Analysis

With one tap, the AI compares the patient's gait against thousands of patterns and generates an instant risk assessment.

04

Act & Refer

Clear risk alerts guide action. High-risk patients are referred to city hospitals for specialist care and X-ray confirmation.

AI Risk Assessment System

Built for Remote Realities

Offline-First Architecture

All data stored locally on the device. Syncs automatically when internet becomes available.

Low-Power Hardware

Efficient sensor design ensures weeks of operation on a single charge.

Secure & Private

End-to-end encryption protects patient health information at every stage.

Health Worker Friendly

  • Affordable hardware makes the solution scalable across thousands of villages
